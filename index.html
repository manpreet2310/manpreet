01Asymmetric solitary waves observed by MMS+

Identified recurrent asymmetric electrostatic waveforms with unequal bipolar peaks and oscillatory tails. Combining waveform statistics, polarization and propagation diagnostics with particle-in-cell calculations and synthetic spacecraft crossings to distinguish intrinsic asymmetry from viewing geometry and constrain nonlinear structure formation.

Progress Observations and initial statistics obtained; kinetic pilot calculations and geometric tests completed; mechanism validation ongoing.

Lead author
02Strong perpendicular wave–particle energy transfer in MMS observations+

Identified intervals of strong perpendicular electric-field–current energy exchange. Using field-aligned decomposition of J·E, plasma-frame diagnostics, wave spectra and particle velocity distributions to assess the role of perpendicular interactions in collisionless energization and distinguish energy exchange from irreversible heating.

Progress Event analysis and diagnostic framework developed; cadence, reference-frame and heating checks ongoing.

Lead author
03Pair starvation and ion loading in pulsar current sheets+

Two related manuscripts. Pair starvation: derived current-support bounds under finite particle-energy constraints and examined how ion loading changes the available current. Tearing instability: developed composition and enthalpy criteria to separate bulk ion inertia from direct current-sheet response. These studies constrain when pulsar-wind sheets can sustain current and when ion loading may affect reconnection.

Progress Analytical models and manuscript drafts prepared; tearing-specific validation ongoing.

Lead author
04Double-layer formation through offset density and temperature gradients+

Developed a one-dimensional fluid–Poisson framework showing how displaced density and temperature profiles break symmetry and permit a finite potential jump. Derived an offset-dependent voltage scaling and generalized the voltage budget to pressure, inertia and current effects, providing testable criteria for electrostatic particle acceleration.

Progress Analytical framework, figures and manuscript draft prepared; numerical validation ongoing.

Lead author
05Radio plasma lensing by MHD shocks+

Combined ion–neutral dispersion analysis, pressure constraints and radio ray tracing to test whether shock-associated plasma sheets can produce extreme scattering events. Calculated lensing light curves and caustic thresholds, identifying a mismatch between assumed sub-AU lens widths and ion–neutral shock scales that constrains viable lens-formation models.

Progress Numerical study and technical draft completed; self-consistent lens formation under investigation.

Lead author
06MMS search for magnetosheath double layers+

Compiled a list of MMS magnetosheath intervals containing double-layer. Applying region identification, electric-field morphology, potential-jump and particle-distribution diagnostics to distinguish double layers from bipolar solitary structures and assess localized electron acceleration in the magnetosheath.

Progress Interval catalogue and search strategy completed; basic properties such as speed, wavelength, and angle of propagation measured with MMS spacecraft; statistical analysis ongoing.

Lead author
07QED-modified inertial Alfvén waves+

Derived a cold multifluid framework coupling vacuum-polarization corrections to finite-inertia Alfvén waves in magnetized electron–positron–ion plasmas. Established leading-order Hall cancellation and examined weak trace-ion mass loading; extended the model to test whether QED frequency shifts can control resonant instability in streaming plasmas near magnetars.

Progress Baseline derivation and extended manuscript draft prepared; resonance and parameter-regime validation ongoing.

Lead author
08Ion-acoustic waves in the interstellar medium+

Investigating the excitation and propagation of ion-acoustic disturbances under interstellar plasma conditions, emphasizing physical existence constraints rather than unrestricted parameter scans. The study uses fluid dispersion analysis and parameter-space mapping to assess how composition, temperature and magnetization govern viable electrostatic modes and their potential role in energy transport.

Progress Theoretical study in progress; applicability constraints and manuscript development ongoing.

Lead author
